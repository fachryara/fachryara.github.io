Welcome! I am Fachry Abdul Razak Afifi, an aspiring economist interested in environmental, climate, and development economics.

I received my M.S. in Agricultural and Applied Economics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison and my B.A. in Economics from Universitas Indonesia. Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Institute for Economic and Social Research (LPEM FEB UI), where I contributed to research on environmental economics, energy transition, climate policy, and natural resource management in Indonesia. I also worked with J-PAL Southeast Asia as a Project Assistant on a randomized controlled trial evaluating Indonesia's smallholder palm oil replanting program.

I would like to pursue research on the design and evaluation of environmental and climate policies, particularly those that reshape economic incentives through markets and institutions. My research focuses on three complementary areas: (i) energy and environmental markets, (ii) climate adaptation and mitigation, and (iii) natural resource governance and property rights. Across these areas, I seek to understand how households, firms, and governments respond to environmental policies and how policy design can improve environmental and economic outcomes, particularly in developing countries.
