Welcome! I am Fachry Abdul Razak Afifi, an aspiring economist interested in environmental, climate, and development economics.

I received my M.S. in Agricultural and Applied Economics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison and my B.A. in Economics from Universitas Indonesia. Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Institute for Economic and Social Research (LPEM FEB UI), where I contributed to research on environmental economics, energy transition, climate policy, and natural resource management in Indonesia. I also worked with J-PAL Southeast Asia as a Project Assistant on a randomized controlled trial evaluating Indonesia's smallholder palm oil replanting program.

I would like to pursue research on the design and evaluation of environmental and climate policies, particularly market-based policy instruments, and on how these policies reshape economic incentives and influence the behavior of households, firms, and governments. More broadly, I am interested in understanding how well-designed environmental policies can achieve environmental objectives while promoting economic efficiency, equitable development, and long-run welfare, particularly in developing countries. Methodologically, I am interested in combining causal inference with geospatial, remote sensing, and administrative data to evaluate the effectiveness of environmental policies and institutions.
