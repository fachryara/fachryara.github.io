I am an aspiring environmental and energy economist, currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Agricultural and Applied Economics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. I hold a B.A. in Economics from Universitas Indonesia. Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Institute for Economic and Social Research, Faculty of Economics and Business, Universitas Indonesia (LPEM FEB UI), where I contributed to research on environmental, energy, and climate economics and policy. I was also involved in a Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT) on evaluating Peremajaan Sawit Rakyat (PSR)/ Palm Oil Replanting programs in Indonesia with the Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab Southeast Asia (JPAL SEA), where I served as a Project Assistant. 

I am interested in environmental, energy, climate, agriculture, and development economics.
